01 / Approach

Technical depth.
Practical judgment.

From enterprise platforms to manuscript preservation, I turn ambiguous problems into working products. My grounding in risk operations and data engineering shapes how I lead: understand the workflow, build dependable systems, and validate with the people who use them.

Discovery + people

Understand the work before designing the product.

I start with people, their tasks, and the institutional constraints around them. Interviews, journey mapping, and in-person workshops help me understand where work slows down and what a useful solution needs to preserve.

For ShrutiLekha, I worked with scholars to understand manuscript transcription end to end, translated those insights into a workflow application, and returned for in-person user testing and validation. At Microsoft, the same human-centred approach shaped self-service onboarding and transaction visibility for both technical and non-technical users.

Strategy + systems

Build foundations that make the next step easier.

My data-engineering background makes me attentive to the dependencies behind an experience: data flows, integrations, operational reliability, and ownership. At Accenture, that meant enterprise migration, hundreds of data pipelines, and responsibility when production systems failed.

At Microsoft, I connect a multi-year platform vision to foundational capabilities, phased releases, and enterprise migration. The goal is a shared foundation for supplier collaboration, not a simultaneous replacement of every tool.

See the platform work ↗

AI + judgment

Use AI to empower.
Let humans lead.

I embed AI where it removes friction in an existing workflow. At Microsoft, a self-service API-testing agent reduced manual effort by approximately 70%. In ShrutiLekha, speech recognition helps scholars turn spoken readings into text, while expert editing and review retain scholarly control over accuracy.

Speed alone is not enough. I use prompt experimentation, evaluations, A/B testing, human feedback, and telemetry to improve quality. Version history, audit trails, and on-premises options in ShrutiLekha reflect the same concern for traceability and institutional needs.

People + delivery

Make alignment tangible.

I turn shared intent into priorities, releases, and an adoption path. At Microsoft, that means aligning four engineering teams and senior stakeholders around a platform roadmap. At Novartis, it meant leading a vendor engineering team and working with 17 pharmaceutical companies and regulatory bodies across four countries.

Taking electronic Product Information from concept through EU rollout reinforced the importance of compliance, repeatable onboarding, and operational ownership. A working product needs more than a launch: partners need a practical way to adopt it and teams need a dependable way to run it.

Learning + community

Learn in public. Share what works.

Co-leading Global AI Hyderabad, speaking about applied AI, and running hands-on sessions keep learning connected to practice. Through the ANRF Ambassador Programme, I regularly attend research-paper reading sessions and evaluate research students' projects at IIIT Hyderabad.

I share experiments and ideas through talks on GitHub Copilot, agent orchestration, evaluations, and multi-agent architectures, and write occasionally at The AI Lantern on Substack. Teaching, discussion, and research are ways to question assumptions, not just reinforce them.

My formal grounding spans the Executive Programme for Young Managers at IIM Calcutta, completed with distinction in 2016; Electronics and Communication Engineering at Guru Nanak Engineering College, JNTU Hyderabad; and Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900).
