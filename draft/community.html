Volunteer work / SCSVMV University

ShrutiLekha: Accelerating the Preservation of India’s Knowledge Heritage

1. The Problem — A Preservation Challenge at Scale

Universities and archives hold vast collections of Indian manuscripts containing invaluable scholarship. Converting these into usable digital text is slow, specialist-intensive work. Manual transcription, complex scripts, and fragmented workflows create a bottleneck—limiting how much knowledge institutions can preserve and make accessible.

2. The Solution — AI-Assisted, Scholar-Validated

ShrutiLekha turns a scholar’s spoken reading into digital text. It brings together multiple speech-recognition engines, manuscript images, expert editing, and review in one end-to-end application. Scholars retain final control over accuracy, while version history and audit trails make the results traceable and trustworthy. Institutional content can remain on-premises.

3. The Impact — Up to 10× Faster Processing

ShrutiLekha enables up to 10× faster transcription than typing-only methods. Workflow automation connects recording, transcription, editing, and finalization, while dashboards make progress visible. This helps institutions handle larger volumes without a proportional increase in manual effort—freeing scholars to focus on interpretation and quality.

4. My Contribution — Leading from Discovery to Validation

I led the initiative from understanding the problem to delivering a working solution. Through in-person workshops, I developed a deep, end-to-end understanding of scholars’ needs and institutional workflows. I translated those insights into a complete workflow application, then conducted in-person user testing and validation to refine it around real scholarly practice—not assumptions.

5. Recognition — University Leadership Acknowledgment

The initiative received recognition from the university’s dean, acknowledging its value and the contribution toward preserving scholarly heritage.

Executive takeaway: ShrutiLekha combines AI speed with scholarly judgment to turn a labor-intensive preservation process into a scalable, accountable digital workflow.
